Clipoa — your short-video studio

Clipoa is a desktop application that helps creators write, edit and render vertical short videos (9:16), then publish or schedule them on YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels from one place.

Clipoa est un studio de bureau pour créer des vidéos courtes verticales et les publier ou les programmer sur YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels et Facebook Reels.

Create

Turn an idea or a long video into a ready-to-post short: script, voice-over, subtitles and editing, rendered on your own computer.

Publish

Connect your accounts with each platform's official sign-in page, then publish a short immediately or schedule it for later.

Several accounts

Connect more than one YouTube channel or TikTok profile and switch the active account in one click. Each scheduled post remembers which account it belongs to.

How Clipoa uses your YouTube account

When you choose “Connect YouTube”, Google asks you to allow Clipoa to:

  • Manage your YouTube videos (youtube.upload) — used only to upload the short videos you select in Clipoa, with the title and description you write, at the time you choose.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly) — used only to show the name of the connected channel so you know which channel a video will be published to.

Clipoa never deletes, edits or reads your existing videos, comments or analytics, and never publishes anything without your action. You can disconnect a channel in Clipoa at any time and revoke access from your Google Account permissions page.

Clipoa uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube account you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy and our Privacy Policy.
