Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026 · Version française plus bas

This policy explains what data Clipoa (“the app”, “we”) accesses, how it is used, stored and shared, and how you can delete it. Contact: brice.mialon01800@gmail.com.

1. Data we access

Google / YouTube user data

If you connect a YouTube account, Clipoa requests these Google OAuth scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — to upload the videos you select in Clipoa to your channel.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly — to read your channel's name and ID, displayed in Clipoa so you can see which channel is connected.

We receive an OAuth access token and refresh token, and your channel name and channel ID. We do not access your email address, contacts, existing videos, comments, analytics or any other Google data.

TikTok, Instagram and Facebook

If you connect these platforms, Clipoa receives the access tokens and basic profile identifiers (username, account or page ID) needed to publish the videos you choose.

Your videos

Videos, scripts and captions you create are stored on your own computer.

2. How we use the data

  • To publish or schedule the videos you choose, to the account you choose, with the title and description you write.
  • To show which account is connected and let you switch between your connected accounts.
  • To refresh access tokens so scheduled posts can be published at the time you set.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or to train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

3. Storage and protection

Tokens and account identifiers are stored locally on your computer, in Clipoa's data folder, in files readable only by your user account. Sign-in may pass through Clipoa's sign-in relay (a Cloudflare Worker) which exchanges the authorization code for tokens and hands them directly back to the app on your computer; the relay does not store tokens or personal data. All transfers use HTTPS.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties. Data is sent only to the platform you are publishing to (for example, the video and its metadata to YouTube when you publish).

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

Clipoa's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Clipoa uses YouTube API Services: see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Data is kept only while the account is connected in Clipoa.
  • Click ✕ next to an account in Clipoa to delete its tokens and identifiers from your computer immediately.
  • You can also revoke Clipoa's access at any time: Google — myaccount.google.com/permissions; TikTok, Instagram and Facebook — in each app's security or connected-apps settings.
  • To ask any question or request deletion, email brice.mialon01800@gmail.com.

7. Changes

We will update this page if our practices change, and update the date at the top.
